Scan, model, print — one process

Some objects have no file and no catalogue entry: a part out of production, a carved ornament on a building, a fitting that was made once and never again. Those start with a scan rather than a drawing.

The object is captured on site by photogrammetry, rebuilt as a measured 3D model, repaired digitally where it has been damaged, and then printed — as the finished piece itself, or as the master for a mould when the final part needs to be plaster, cast stone or metal. The model is yours either way.

Captured examples are in the scan library, and the wider surveying and modelling work is described on the CAD-to-BIM and scan-to-BIM page.

Equipment

Bambu Lab P1P with AMS. Build volume 256 × 256 × 256 mm, up to four colours in a single print. Materials are PLA and PETG: PLA for display pieces, signage and figurines, PETG where the part has to take heat, moisture or load.

ABS and ASA are not offered — the printer is open-frame, and those materials need a heated enclosure to come out sound. Larger objects are printed in sections and joined.

Questions

I don't have a 3D file. Can you still print it?

Yes. Send photos of the part with a ruler or tape measure in frame, or bring the broken piece itself. Modelling from a sample or a photograph is part of the replacement part service and is included in the quoted price.

How big can you print, and in what material?

Up to 256 × 256 × 256 mm in one piece, in PLA or PETG, with up to four colours in a single print. Larger objects are printed in sections and joined. PETG is the choice for anything that sees heat, moisture or load.

What does a job cost, and is there a minimum?

Prices start at $35 for a plaque or house number and $45 for a replacement part. The minimum order is $40. You get a fixed quote before anything is printed, so the price does not move after the fact.

How long does it take?

Two to four days for most parts, five to ten days for a production run. Rush work in 24 to 48 hours is available at a 50% surcharge, subject to the queue.

Do you deliver, or do I pick up?

Pickup in Sunset Park is free. Delivery anywhere in the listed Brooklyn service area is a flat $15.
