Privacy Policy – FE Civil Prep

Developer: Zakhar Galanin (zakhargalan.in) · Last updated: August 10, 2026

FE Civil Prep is an independent study tool for the FE Civil exam. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Short version: no account is required, we don't know your name or email, we show no ads, and we never sell data.

01Data We Collect

  • Random device identifier. On first launch the app generates a random identifier stored only on your device. It is not derived from your name, email, phone number, or any Apple identifier. We use it to count free AI-tutor usage per day and to associate an active subscription with your device.
  • AI tutor content. When you use the AI tutor, the practice problem, your chosen answer, and your chat messages are sent to our server and processed by Anthropic's API to generate a response. This content is used to answer your question and is not used to build a profile of you.
  • Usage counters. Our server keeps a per-device count of AI requests per day to enforce the free plan limit, and standard short-lived technical logs.
  • Subscription status. Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether a device has an active subscription. We never see your payment details.

02Data We Do NOT Collect

Name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifiers, or tracking across other companies' apps and websites. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Your study progress and statistics are stored only on your device.

03Third Parties We Rely On

  • Anthropic — processes AI tutor requests (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription status (privacy policy)
  • Apple — payment processing for subscriptions

04Retention and Deletion

AI chat content is processed to generate a response and retained only in short-lived operational logs. Daily usage counters reset each day. Deleting the app removes your device identifier and all local progress. To request deletion of any server-side records associated with your device identifier (shown in the app under Settings), contact us at the email below.

05Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional engineering licensure exam and is not directed to children under 13.
